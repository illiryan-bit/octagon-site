Questions
What is Octagon?+

Octagon is a founder-led studio that builds and runs AI agent systems for the operational work of companies and brands: online store, Amazon, SEO, social media, ad campaigns, photo and video, customer service. The system prepares the work; every action that matters goes out only after your approval on Telegram.

Which platforms do you work with?+

Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, Vinted and Grailed; Google and AI assistants; Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn; Meta, Google, TikTok and Amazon Ads; Klaviyo for email. If you use different tools, we check them on the call before we start.

How long does it take to get started?+

It starts with a 20-minute call and a single repetitive task. With the Starter package, setup takes 3–5 days; with the larger packages, we handle the configuration for you.

Do I have to change the tools I use?+

No. Octagon works on the channels and tools you already have. You talk to the system on Telegram.

What happens if the AI gets it wrong?+

First, a rule written in code stops it, such as a minimum price or a spending limit. Then every action with a consequence waits for your OK. Nothing goes out without you.

Where do the workflows run, and where does the data stay?+

On infrastructure managed by Octagon: orchestration, databases and memory are self-hosted. On the call, we explain in detail what goes through external services.

Do you also work with large companies or retail networks?+

Yes. The same system serves a single store and a brand with partners in several countries: each location has its own rules, and the brand stays one.

Photo and video: where do they come from?+

We create them in-house, on our own infrastructure, starting from your photos and your brand's visual guidelines. No subscription to external services for every image.

Do you also build websites and online stores?+

Yes. Landing pages, Shopify stores and custom applications, connected to the same system. And we run your Amazon store in the same system.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on what the system takes on. You'll find the packages in our price list.
