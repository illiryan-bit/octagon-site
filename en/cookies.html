01

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser to remember information between pages or between visits. Other tools work in a similar way, such as the browser's local storage (localStorage), tracking pixels, and fonts or videos loaded from other sites. On this page we call them all “cookies”.

02

Which cookies this site uses

None. theoctagonai.com does not set cookies or store information in your browser's storage: no technical cookies, no analytics cookies, no profiling or advertising cookies.

CategoryUsed?Notes
Technical and sessionNoThe site works without remembering anything about you.
Statistics (analytics)NoNo visitor measurement tools.
Profiling and advertisingNoNo social network or ad platform pixels.
Third-party contentNoFonts, images and code are all hosted on our own domain.

That's why you don't see a cookie banner: under the Italian Data Protection Authority's Guidelines of June 10, 2021, consent is required only for non-technical cookies, and there are none here.

03

Third-party services

The site does not embed videos, maps, social buttons or fonts loaded from other servers. The only provider involved when you visit a page is Vercel, which hosts the site and logs technical data for each request, such as the IP address, to run and protect it. Vercel does not set cookies on the public pages of this site. Details are in theprivacy policy.

If you follow a link to another site or service, such as Telegram or WhatsApp, its own cookie rules apply.

04

How you can check

You don't have to take our word for it. Open your browser's developer tools (F12 on a computer), go to Application or Storage and check the Cookies and Local Storage entries for theoctagonai.com: they are empty. In the Network tab you'll see only requests to our own domain.

05

How to manage cookies in your browser

Even though this site doesn't use any, you can block or delete cookies for all sites in your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Safari
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
06

If this ever changes

If in the future we add tools that use non-technical cookies, such as non-anonymous statistics or advertising pixels, we will first update this page and ask for your consent with a banner, letting you refuse as easily as you accept. Version in force from October 7, 2026.
