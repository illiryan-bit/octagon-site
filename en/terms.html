How the agreement is formed

04

Automation services

How payment works

Automation packages have a one-time setup fee, for design and configuration, followed by a monthly fee for operation, monitoring and maintenance. Add-ons are activated and deactivated on a monthly basis.

Term and cancellation

The monthly fee has a minimum term of three months. After the first three months you can cancel whenever you like, in the manner and with the notice stated in the proposal. The setup fee covers work already done and is non-refundable.

You stay in control

Octagon's systems prepare the work and ask for your approval before every action that matters: publishing, sending, changing a price, spending. Approved actions are your decisions: check what is proposed to you before approving it. AI-generated content may contain errors, and that is exactly why it goes through you.

The agreed deterministic checks, such as minimum prices, spending limits and brand rules, are written in code, and we keep them active for the entire duration of the service.
