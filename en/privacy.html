At a glance

  • The site uses no cookies and no tracking or advertising tools.
  • We collect data from the demo form only to reply to you and prepare a proposal.
  • We don't sell data and we don't use it for marketing without separate consent.
  • Our US providers (Vercel, Resend) are certified under the Data Privacy Framework.
  • You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your data.
01

Who processes your data

The data controller is whoever decides why and how your personal data is used. For this website and for Octagon's services, the controller is:

Data controller
Edison Zoicaj
Email
info@theoctagonai.com

Octagon is a small studio and is not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO). For any request about your data, write to the email address above: the controller will reply to you directly.

02

What data we collect

When you visit the site

The server hosting the site automatically logs some technical data for each request: IP address, date and time, requested page, browser and device type, and any errors. This data is needed to run the site and protect it from abuse. We don't use it to profile you and we don't combine it with other data.

The site does not use cookies, does not use analytics or advertising tools, and does not load resources from third-party services: even the fonts are hosted on our own domain. Details are in the cookie policy.

When you request a demo

With the “Book a demo” form you send us your name and your work email, which are required, and optionally the name of your business and a description of the repetitive work. Please do not include other people's personal data or special categories of data (health, political or religious opinions and the like) in the description: we don't need them to understand your case.

When you write to us or become a client

If you contact us by email, WhatsApp or Telegram, we process the data you share with us and the data needed to reply. If you become a client, we also process data for invoicing and contract management (name or company name, VAT number, address, contact details of the people who work with us).

04

Do you have to give us your data?

No. You don't need to provide anything to browse the site. Name and email are needed only if you want us to reply to your demo request: without them we can't get back to you. The other form fields are optional.

05

Who else sees the data

Data is processed by Octagon. For some activities we rely on providers that process it on our behalf, only for the service requested and under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR):

  • Vercel Inc. (United States): hosts the site and logs technical browsing data.
  • Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., United States): forwards the requests submitted through the demo form to Octagon by email.
  • The provider of our mailbox, where we receive and store correspondence.
  • Telegram, only if you choose to write to us there: in that case Telegram's privacy policy also applies.
  • WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Limited, Meta group), only if you choose to write to us there using the button on the site: in that case WhatsApp's privacy policy also applies.
  • Our accountant, for client billing data only.

We may disclose data to authorities only when required by law.

06

Transfers outside the European Union

Vercel and Resend are based in the United States. Both are certified under theEU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, recognized as adequate by the European Commission in its decision of July 10, 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR). You can verify the certification on the official website dataprivacyframework.gov.

07

How long we keep it

  • Technical browsing data: for the limited period set by the hosting provider's logs, then deleted automatically.
  • Demo requests and correspondence that don't lead to a contract: 12 months from the last contact, then deleted.
  • Contract and billing data: 10 years from the end of the financial year, as required by law (Art. 2220 of the Italian Civil Code).

If you ask us to delete your data before these deadlines, we will, except for data the law requires us to keep.

08

Data we process on behalf of clients

When Octagon's systems work for a client (for example, replying to the client's customers, updating listings or preparing emails), they may process personal data of third parties. For that data the controller is the client, and Octagon acts as data processor (Art. 28 GDPR), under a written agreement that specifies the data, purposes, security measures and providers involved.

Actions that matter require a person's approval before they are carried out, and the checks on prices, spending limits and rules are written in code.

09

Your rights

At any time you can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we process your data and receive a copy of it (access, Art. 15);
  • correct or complete it (rectification, Art. 16);
  • delete it (Art. 17) or restrict its use (Art. 18);
  • receive it in a machine-readable format or have it transmitted to another controller (portability, Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (objection, Art. 21).

Write to info@theoctagonai.com. We reply free of charge within one month, as required by the GDPR. To protect your data, we may ask you to confirm your identity.

If you believe the processing breaches the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or with the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live or work.

10

How we protect data

The site is served over HTTPS only. Access to data is limited to people who work for Octagon and need it, with personal credentials and multi-factor authentication where available. We keep the bare minimum and delete what is no longer needed.

11

Children

The site and services are intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 14: if we find we have received any, we delete it.

12

Changes to this notice

If we change the way we process data, for example by adding a new provider, we update this page and the date at the top. We also flag important changes on the site. Version in force from October 7, 2026.
